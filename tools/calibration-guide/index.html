2 Capture Dark Frames

Dark frames reveal the sensor's fixed-pattern noise (DSNU), read noise, and row/column banding.

⚠ Body cap must be ON. No light should reach the sensor. Tape over any viewfinder/EVF light leaks.

3 Capture Flat Field Frames

Flat fields reveal PRNU (Photo Response Non-Uniformity) — the per-pixel gain variation.

Setup: Point the camera at an evenly-illuminated white surface. A light box, overcast sky, or white wall with diffused lighting works. Deliberately defocus to avoid texture.

What to shoot:

ISOExposureFramesNotes
64Auto (mid-range)50Aim for ~50% histogram
200Auto (mid-range)50Same illumination
800Auto (mid-range)50Same illumination
⚠ Avoid clipping. The histogram should not touch the right edge. Reduce exposure if needed. Flat fields with clipped highlights are useless for PRNU calibration.

4 Run Calibration

The calibrate tool processes your dark frames and flat fields into a sensor noise model.

This will output:

  • DSNU map (per-pixel dark signal non-uniformity)
  • Row/column banding offsets
  • PRNU gain map (if flat fields provided)
  • Per-channel noise statistics

5 Test Noise-Aware Compression

Encode a photo with the sensor calibration and measure the improvement.

Expected Results:

ModeCompressionNotes
Normal2-6xBaseline (no noise correction)
Denoise only4-12xPhase A wavelet thresholding
Full calibration5-15xDSNU + PRNU + wavelet thresholding

The compression ratio is the objective quality metric. Higher ratio = more noise entropy identified and separated. The decoded image faithfully reproduces the original (including noise) by default.

🎉 Calibration Complete!

Your sensor calibration file can be reused for all future photos from this camera. Store it alongside your images or in a central calibration directory.
